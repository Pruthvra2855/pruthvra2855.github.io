A Biophysical Fusion Framework for Species-Conditioned Antibacterial MIC Prediction

Timeline: 2024 — 2026 · BRAC University

Developed a multimodal predictive machine learning framework fusing molecular biophysical descriptors with species-specific genomic embeddings to estimate antibacterial Minimum Inhibitory Concentration (MIC). Designed custom feature-extraction pipelines to process high-dimensional biological data while mitigating multicollinearity.

Biological Fusion: Species-conditioned genomic embeddings combined with molecular descriptors.
Feature Engineering: Multicollinearity reduction across high-dimensional datasets.
